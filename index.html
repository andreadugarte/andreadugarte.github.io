DP World grew revenue 13.1% in H1 2026 and earned US$170m less EBITDA. Here is where the money went.

I took the company's own H1 2026 results release, rebuilt the numbers in a spreadsheet, split the EBITDA change into a revenue effect and a margin effect, and mapped where AI could take routine reporting work off the Finance, Legal, Commercial, Operations and Business Development teams.

The finding

Revenue growth alone would have added about US$397m of EBITDA. A lower margin on this year's revenue took away about US$567m. Net: down US$170m.

Hypothesis, not a finding The release does not split margin by terminal, so I cannot say why the margin fell. Two explanations fit the numbers and are worth testing: volume lost at a high-margin terminal (Jebel Ali throughput fell while the rest of the network grew), or faster growth in lower-margin logistics. The release's segment table is where to test this, and I have not yet checked it line by line.

  1. A Claude workflow turns the results release into a variance brief with every figure quoted.
  2. A separate agent that saw only the official release, not my notes, wrote its own brief. This keeps my own extraction from grading itself.
  3. A script checks each reported figure against the source text and recomputes each derived figure (growth rates, margins) on its own.
  4. The altered-figure test confirms the script catches an error.

I have not measured time saved, because there is no manual baseline yet. That is the first thing a pilot at DP World would set up.
